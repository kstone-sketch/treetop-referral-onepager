Referral One-Pager

One printed page that shows a referring doctor where every child they sent us stands. A rep presses a button in Salesforce, hands this to the physician on a visit, or attaches it to an email as a PDF. Styled to match thetreetop.com.

Fits on one page

US Letter, fixed height. No scrolling, no second sheet, nothing cut off at the fold.

Three columns

In Care, In Progress, Needs Attention. Detailed status sits beside the name in the last two.

Safer than an email

Names travel as a PDF attachment rather than in an email body.

Where each child lands

This is the mapping Dove builds against. Every child in the six-month window resolves to exactly one column and one printed label. Column one prints names only, since a doctor does not need a status for a child already in therapy.

Row markup to generate

Each ROWS_* field holds a block of these, one per child. Columns two and three carry a status; column one does not.

</div>

Before it ships

Printing and limits

  • Ten names per column with a status, fifteen withoutMeasured, not estimated: the In Progress and Needs Attention columns overflow the sheet at the eleventh name, In Care at the sixteenth. A doctor above that needs a rule. My recommendation is to print the first ten and close the column with a "plus 4 more, ask us for the full list" line, which keeps the promise of one page. Confirm with Ethan and I will build it.
  • The status legend can be trimmed per doctorThe definitions under the columns explain every status. If a given doctor has nobody in a status, that definition does not need to print, which buys back room for names. Worth doing if the overflow rule turns out to bite often.
  • Background graphics must be onThe purple header and colored column bars are backgrounds. If the PDF step renders with them off, the page turns white. Chrome headless honors print-color-adjust: exact, which is already set.
  • Fonts load from GoogleInstrument Sans and Unna come over the network. If the renderer is offline, Georgia and the system sans stand in and the layout still holds, but it will not match the site.
  • Skip empty generationsIf a doctor has nobody in the window, do not produce the page.
  • It carries PHIChild names with treatment status. Compliance should sign off on the Salesforce button, who can press it, and where the file is stored, before the first real one goes out.
