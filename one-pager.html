What each status means

In care: Therapy is underway and going well. We will keep you posted on new milestones, and the assigned BCBA is always glad to talk through progress.

In assessment: Assessment is in progress and the care plan is being prepared for insurance submission. This is the last step before therapy begins.

Verifying benefits and finalizing paperwork: We are in regular contact with the family and are collecting the last documentation needed to move forward.

Matching a BCBA: We are matching the right clinic and the right BCBA, and watching capacity closely so we can move the moment an opening comes up.

Needs a diagnosis or insurance review: Either there is no autism diagnosis on file yet, in which case we help the family find a provider who can complete one, or we are still working through insurance.

Unable to reach family: We have reached out and have not connected yet. We will keep trying, and will call you directly if we continue to have trouble.

Services paused: This can follow a change in coverage, a family request, or a temporary change such as a pending restart, reschedule, or transfer to another clinic.
